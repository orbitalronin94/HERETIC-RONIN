## Cosmopedia v0.1: bounded curation

Pinned source:
- Dataset: [HuggingFaceTB/cosmopedia](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceTB/cosmopedia)
- Revision: `0ae6ec63f91742bd2d1eaef4f02232c55d719385`
- Config/split: `auto_math_text/train`
- Dataset card declaration: Apache-2.0
- Extracted payload SHA-256: `050b03f64c3e9976d91ff86447761174f46121c0af899004172368f61255dcc6`

The source card describes Cosmopedia as synthetic text generated by Mixtral-8x7B-Instruct-v0.1, with seed samples from other datasets/external sources. The reviewed config is specifically `auto_math_text`. The upstream seed lineage is therefore retained as a caveat; the dataset-card declaration is recorded as publisher evidence, not a legal opinion or an independent clearance of every upstream seed.

Mechanical curation:
- Input: 5,000 train rows.
- Kept: **4,734**.
- Rejected: **266** — 265 records containing control/replacement characters and one over the 12,000-character cap.
- No email or phone-pattern hits were found in this 5,000-row slice.
- No exact duplicate remained after normalized-text hashing.
- Every retained record keeps source, immutable revision, config, declared license, seed-data/audience/format metadata and a SHA-256 of its text.
- Retained text is educational prose only; it is not treated as mathematically verified or as instruction/answer pairs.

Manual sample review is a spot check, not a proof of correctness. It does not certify every record or every equation. This is why the source is still **not authorized for training** at this stage.

## Global gate

The new workflow `.github/workflows/audit-cosmopedia-candidate.yml`:
1. Downloads the exact existing RONIN-IA train-only bundle and candidate artifact by immutable artifact IDs.
2. Verifies both archive SHA-256 values.
3. Rebuilds the 4,734-row curated candidate from the pinned bundle.
4. Adds those rows to the same global exact-duplicate, SimHash near-duplicate and 120-question diagnostic holdout audit used for the current training corpus.
5. Publishes the curated data, curation manifest and global audit report together.

The global audit script was also corrected so exact-duplicate exclusion coverage is tracked for **all** duplicate endpoints, not only the first 200 examples displayed in the report.

**Training remains blocked** until the global audit reports `training_authorized: true`, complete near-duplicate coverage, and zero exact holdout question matches. Even then, the source is admitted only as experimental next-token pretraining text; it does not become a benchmark, gold-answer or reasoning-supervision source.

## Why the other sources are not admitted

- [FineWeb-Edu](https://huggingface.co/datasets/HuggingFaceFW/fineweb-edu) and [OpenWebMath](https://huggingface.co/datasets/open-web-math/open-web-math) are database-level open collections derived from web pages. Their database licenses do not settle rights in every underlying page. The sample itself includes third-party publisher and forum material, so we do not treat ODC-By as a blanket content license.
- Cosmopedia v2 is kept separate from v0.1. Its mixed source lineage and repeated synthetic templates make it a poorer first admission candidate, especially while the current corpus is still being expanded.

## Accounting rule

Only bytes in a source artifact that has passed source review, curation, integrity verification, global deduplication and holdout-contamination checks may be counted as **admitted training bytes**. Downloaded candidate bytes and quarantined bytes remain separate.
